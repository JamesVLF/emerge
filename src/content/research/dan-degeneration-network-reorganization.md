Burst-to-burst correlation analysis between neurons fails when studying the effects of neurotoxins at the circuit level. Since destruction of neurons destroys correlation, connective patterns that may exist at lower levels in the organizational hierarchy of circuits would be missed. Dimensionality reduction overcomes this limitation by showing how and if neural networks reorganize after dopaminergic neuron degeneration.
